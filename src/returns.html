Under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act you may cancel your order for any reason, without penalty, within 7 days after you receive it. Faulty or not as described

Under the Consumer Protection Act (sections 55 and 56), if the product is defective, unsafe or not as described within 6 months of delivery, you may return it at our cost and risk. You then choose a repair, a replacement or a refund. Send us a photo and your order reference so we can arrange it quickly.
