Why a heatless curling set

No heat tools

The curl forms as your hair dries overnight, so there's no hot iron and no heat-protect spray step.

Sleep-friendly satin

A soft, flexible foam rod with a smooth satin-style cover, plus two scrunchies to hold your hair.

Five minutes at night

Wrapping takes a few minutes once you've done it a couple of times. Your morning is just: unwrap and go.

How it works

  1. Clip the rod on

    Lay the rod over the top of your head and secure the middle with a claw clip or hair clip (use your own; one is not included).

  2. Wrap slightly damp hair

    Take sections and twist them around the rod on each side, working down. Damp, not soaking, works best.

  3. Tie off and sleep

    Secure each end with a scrunchie and go to bed.

  4. Unwrap and shake out

    Slide the rod out in the morning and loosen the waves with your fingers.

Results vary with hair length, thickness and texture. Longer hair (shoulder length and below) gets the most visible waves.
