Why go heatless

Curls that form while you sleep

No heat tools

The curl sets as your hair dries overnight, so there's no hot iron and no heat-protect spray step.

Soft enough to sleep in

A flexible foam rod with a soft cloth cover, plus two scrunchies to hold your hair in place.

Five minutes at night

Wrapping takes a few minutes once you've done it a couple of times. In the morning you unwrap and go.

How it works

Four steps, mostly asleep

  1. Clip the rod on

    Lay the rod over the top of your head and secure the middle with the hair clip from the set.

  2. Wrap damp hair

    Twist sections around the rod on each side, working down. Damp, not soaking, works best.

  3. Tie off and sleep

    Secure each end with a scrunchie and go to bed.

  4. Unwrap and shake out

    Slide the rod out in the morning and loosen the waves with your fingers.
